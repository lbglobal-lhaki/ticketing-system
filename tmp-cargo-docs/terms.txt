9. Australian Biosecurity
Australia has strict biosecurity requirements.
Food, plants, seeds, timber, bamboo, animal products and other biological
materials may be prohibited, require permits or be subject to inspection or
treatment.
Customers sending goods Bhutan → Australia must declare all such products
accurately before cargo is accepted.
Australian authorities recommend checking the Biosecurity Import Conditions
system (BICON) before sending relevant goods.
10. Prohibited and Restricted Goods
Customers must not send prohibited, illegal, undeclared or dangerous goods.
Your existing agreement already prohibits accepting dangerous, prohibited,
restricted, live-animal, perishable and other special cargo without the necessary
prior approval. 
Restricted goods will only be accepted where all necessary permits, licences,
declarations and approvals have been obtained and L&B Global has expressly
agreed to accept the shipment.
11. Dangerous Goods
Dangerous goods must not be included in ordinary cargo without prior written
approval.
Examples include explosives, fireworks, compressed gases, fuel, flammable
liquids, certain chemicals, corrosive substances and certain batteries/battery-
powered equipment.
Australian aviation rules prohibit or restrict numerous dangerous goods aboard
aircraft, including certain lithium batteries and flammable liquids. 
If unsure, the customer must disclose the item before dropping it off.
